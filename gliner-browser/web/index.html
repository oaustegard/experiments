Find names in text, for entity types you choose

Type the kinds of things you want found (person, company, drug, bird species, anything) and a small named-entity model marks them in the text. The model runs on your GPU inside this tab; your text is not sent anywhere.

How it works

The model is knowledgator/gliner-bi-edge-v2.0 (Apache-2.0), a GLiNER bi-encoder: one small encoder reads the text, another reads each entity type you type, and a span head scores every run of up to 12 words against every type. Packed with 4-bit and 8-bit weights it downloads as 40 MB (49 MB uncompressed) and is cached by the browser. On the GPU, through WebGPU: the text encoder and the span head's per-word layers and span scores. In JavaScript: tokenising, encoding the entity types (once per type), and picking the non-overlapping spans above the threshold. Nothing leaves the browser except the requests that fetch Bluesky posts.
