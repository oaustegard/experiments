EmbeddingGemma 2 with a fused WebGPU tail

The model runs in onnxruntime-web on WebGPU. Its output buffer stays on the GPU and is encoded by RemexTail into 2-bit codes (remex, seed 42) and searched with an asymmetric scan. No embedding is read back to the CPU on the encode path. The only readback before encoding is a 768-float sum that sets the centring mean.
