Search the AI list, on your GPU

Semantic search over recent posts by the members of the Artificial (and real) Intelligence list. The page fetches the posts from Bluesky, embeds every one with EmbeddingGemma 2 on your GPU, encodes them to 2-bit codes on the GPU, and keeps the index in this browser. No server is involved. The first visit takes a while; search works as soon as the first posts are indexed.
