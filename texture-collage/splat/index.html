Splat Collage

Cuts a picture into colour regions and fills each one with a texture made of 512 soft-edged splats by Splat Diffusion, a small diffusion model that runs on your GPU. The whole collage is one shader with no pixels in it: zoom in as far as you like, and export it as a single WGSL or HTML file. Needs WebGPU. Source and measurements · pixel version
