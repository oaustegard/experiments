Edgewalk: is it a sandwich?

Keep the model unsure. Change as much as you can.
How to play

A model has opinions about what counts as a sandwich. You start with a food it can't decide on. Change the food one piece at a time and keep the model stuck. The more of the food you have changed, the more each move scores.

  • Tap a piece (what holds it, the filling, the sauce, what the menu calls it…), then tap a new value. That is one move.
  • Edge, 45–55% sandwich: the move scores 200 × how far the food has changed. Fence, 35–65%: 100 ×.
  • Slipping, 15–35% or 65–85%: the move scores nothing, and your next move must bring it back inside 35–65% or the model decides.
  • Decided, below 15% or above 85%: the game ends.
  • The piece you just changed is locked for one turn. You have 12 moves and 3 probes: press Probe, then tap a value to see where it would land without making the move.
  • Daily is the same starting food for everyone today; Random draws a new one. Best is your top score in this browser.
