# effort-advisor

Study of whether a per-turn helper that picks effort, model and delegation is worth building, and whether an Ettin spike on the M1 Neural Engine is warranted for it. Result and data summary are in `RESULTS.md`.

Contents: `extract_turns.py`, `analyze.py`, `scripts/` (extraction, signals, ledger, sampling), `replay/` (replay builder and renderer), `hooktest/` (hook harness and findings), `turn-advisor/` (the shadow-mode advisor hook and its tests), `rubric.md`.

Raw transcripts, the extracted turn data, judge outputs and replay prompts are not committed: they contain session content.
