Animal Diffusion

A 23M-parameter diffusion model with self-attention, in hand-written WGSL, running on your GPU. It was trained on the 15,803 close-up animal-face photos of AFHQv2 (cats, dogs, and wild animals such as lions, tigers, foxes and wolves) and makes 64×64 images; the large square shows one scaled up.
