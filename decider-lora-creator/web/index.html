Decision spike creator

Define a decision as questions with fixed options, label some cases, and train a specialist ("spike") for Ettin-17m on your GPU. Ettin-17m is the frozen 10 MB decision model behind the Spiky Ball Decider. The spike is a 0.5 MB adapter (LoRA weights for the encoder plus a small decision head that scores each option) and loads into that page as it is. Nothing leaves the browser; training needs WebGPU and an 11 MB model download.

1. Decision

Prepare a dataset with an AI agent

A spike is only as good as its cases. Copy this prompt into the AI agent you use (Claude, ChatGPT, or a coding agent with your files). It asks about your decision, helps you collect and label cases, and checks the file. Paste what it returns below. If you have drafted questions here, the prompt includes them.

The agent's JSON file:

The demo is the invoice-review workflow from the Typed Decisions benchmark (synthetic cases with five questions each). It starts from the mean of the library's three other business-workflow spikes, leaving the invoice spike out, to show how fast a new spike learns from labels.

Decision as JSON

Typed Decisions shape: {"id", "description", "questions": {"name": {"type": "choice|noul|score", "instructions", "criteria"}}}. Criteria are {"option": "description"} for choice, an array of level descriptions for score (levels 0, 1, …), and optionally {"true", "false"} for noul, a statement the model judges true or false.

2. Cases

Add a case
Add many (JSON lines)

One case per line: {"id": "c1", "state": "…", "gold": {"question": "option"}}. A gold value can also be a distribution, {"option": 0.7, "other": 0.3}. Add "split": "test" to hold a case out.

3. Train

Start from

The spike's starting weights: one library spike, or the mean of several. A mean is cut to rank 8 by SVD, as adapter-library does for its library start.

What trains



LoRA training runs on ettin-wgsl-train's WebGPU backward pass (gradients checked against PyTorch). It needs a few hundred MB of GPU memory. Its LoRA covers Wqkv, Wo, Wi and Wo_mlp.

Stop pauses a run, and Resume continues it exactly where it stopped. The run is also saved in this browser after every epoch and every 50 steps. If the page reloads or the GPU is lost, it can resume from there. Closing a laptop's lid still puts it to sleep.

4. Results

Train to see test accuracy against the start spike (before any training) and the prior (each question's average answer in the training cases).

6. Export

Two files: a .json with the questions and calibration, and a 0.5 MB .bin of adapter weights. They load in the Spiky Ball Decider as they are: put both in one of its sets' spikes/ directories and add the spike to that set's system.json, using the entry shown after download.
