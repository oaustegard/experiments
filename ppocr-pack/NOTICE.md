## PP-OCRv6 models (Apache-2.0)

The weights in `web/det/models/` and `web/rec/models/` are PaddlePaddle's PP-OCRv6 tiny and small text detectors and
recognizers (https://huggingface.co/PaddlePaddle/PP-OCRv6_tiny_det_onnx and `_small_det_`, `_tiny_rec_`, `_small_rec_`
in the same namespace; PaddlePaddle, Apache License 2.0), downloaded by `run/fetch_models.py`. The changes: BatchNorm
is folded into the preceding convolution and the weights are rounded to int8 codes with one fp16 scale per output
channel (`run/export_det.py`, `run/export_rec.py`), then re-laid out as an op program for the WebGPU kernels.
`web/rec/models/*/dict.txt` is the character dictionary from the models' `inference.yml`. Distributed under
Apache-2.0 with this notice of modification; the full text is in `LICENSE-APACHE-2.0` in the repository's
`tesseract-gpu/` directory (the same licence).

## PaddleOCR post-processing (Apache-2.0)

`web/pipeline.js` and `run/ref_pipeline.py` follow PaddleOCR's `DBPostProcess`, `sorted_boxes`,
`get_rotate_crop_image` and `resize_norm_img` (https://github.com/PaddlePaddle/PaddleOCR, Apache License 2.0), and the
CTC decoding in `web/rec/engine.js` follows its `CTCLabelDecode`. The JavaScript reimplements the OpenCV and Clipper
operations they use (contours, minimum-area rectangle, polygon offset, perspective warp). `web/det/` is adapted from
`tesseract-gpu/web/det/` in this repository.

## Test data

The page's two sample images (`web/samples/`) are generated by `run/make_samples.py` from original text. The accuracy
sweep reads FUNSD (non-commercial research use) and SROIE line crops and synthetic lines that `tesseract-gpu/`
generates; none of them are committed.
