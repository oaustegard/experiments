Play a steel plate with your hands

A fingertip that taps the air and stops strikes a virtual resonator where it stops: a row of eight bells across the frame, or a steel plate the shape of the frame. Every mode of the plate is a separate damped sinusoid summed on your GPU, a few thousand of them for each 10 ms block of sound. "Motion impacts" plays with anything that moves fast and stops (a hand, a pen); tapping the screen strikes too.

Sound plays even with the silent switch on where the browser allows it; headphones are best, since the speaker can feed back into the camera's motion.
