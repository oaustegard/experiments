Play a steel plate with your hands

Movement in front of the camera strikes a virtual resonator where it happens: a plate the shape of the frame, or a row of eight bells. Every mode of the plate is a separate damped sinusoid summed on your GPU, a few thousand of them for each 10 ms block of sound. Tapping the screen strikes too.

Sound plays even with the silent switch on where the browser allows it; headphones are best, since the speaker can feed back into the camera's motion.
