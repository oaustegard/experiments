## Getting started

Open https://austegard.com/fun-and-games/tilt-rally.html on a phone with a motion sensor to race by tilting the phone. Lock your phone's screen rotation in portrait before you start, because the game prompts you to turn the phone to landscape itself. On iOS, allow motion access when the browser asks.

### Setup

Hold the phone in portrait on the first screen to choose your track, laps and difficulty. Three tracks ship: Fjord, Quarry and Ring.

### Controls

Turn the phone to landscape when prompted and hold it at the angle you want as neutral, because the game zeroes your tilt when the start lights go out. Tilt left and right to steer. Tilt the top of the phone away from you to accelerate, and tilt it towards you to brake.
