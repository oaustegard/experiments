## Getting started

Tilt Rally runs in a phone browser at https://austegard.com/fun-and-games/tilt-rally.html and steers by tilting the phone. You need a phone with a motion sensor; on iOS, allow motion access when prompted. Lock your phone's screen rotation to portrait before opening the link, because the game prompts you to turn the phone to landscape itself.

### Setup and calibration

Hold the phone in portrait on the first screen to choose the track (Fjord, Quarry, or Ring), the number of laps, and the difficulty. Follow the prompt to turn the phone to landscape, and hold it at the neutral angle you want to race from until the start lights go out, which zeroes the steering calibration.

### Controls

Tilting the phone left and right steers. Tilting the top edge of the phone away from you accelerates, and tilting the top edge towards you brakes.
