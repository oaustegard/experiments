## Getting started

Tilt Rally runs at https://austegard.com/fun-and-games/tilt-rally.html on a phone with a motion sensor, where you pick your race options in portrait and turn the phone to landscape when prompted to drive by tilting. You should lock screen rotation in portrait before starting, because the game asks for landscape itself with a prompt to turn the phone. On iOS, you must allow motion access when asked.

The first screen holds the options for track, laps and difficulty in portrait. Three tracks ship: Fjord, Quarry and Ring. Once you turn the phone to landscape at the prompt, the game zeroes steering calibration to the phone's current tilt when the start lights go out.

Tilting the phone left and right steers. Tilting the top of the phone away from you accelerates. Tilting the top of the phone towards you brakes.
