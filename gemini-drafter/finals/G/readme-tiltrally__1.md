## Getting started

Tilt Rally runs in a phone browser at https://austegard.com/fun-and-games/tilt-rally.html, and you steer it by tilting the phone. You need a phone with a motion sensor; on iOS, allow motion access when prompted. Lock your phone's screen rotation to portrait before opening the link, because the game prompts you to turn the phone to landscape itself.

### Setup and calibration

Hold the phone in portrait on the first screen to choose the track (Fjord, Quarry or Ring), the number of laps and the difficulty. Then follow the prompt to turn the phone to landscape. When the start lights go out, the game zeroes the steering at the phone's current tilt, so hold it at the neutral angle you want to race from until then.

### Controls

Tilt the phone left and right to steer. Tilt the top edge away from you to accelerate, and towards you to brake.
