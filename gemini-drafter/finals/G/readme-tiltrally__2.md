## Getting started

Tilt Rally runs in the browser at https://austegard.com/fun-and-games/tilt-rally.html and needs a phone with a motion sensor. On iOS, allow motion access when the game asks for it.

Lock screen rotation in portrait before you start. The game asks for landscape itself, with a prompt to turn the phone, and an unlocked screen would rotate on its own.

The first screen is held in portrait and has the race options: track, laps and difficulty. Three tracks ship: Fjord, Quarry and Ring. When the prompt appears, turn the phone to landscape. The game zeroes steering to the phone's tilt at the moment the start lights go out, so hold the phone the way you want to drive when they do.

Tilt the phone left and right to steer. Tilt the top of the phone away from you to accelerate and towards you to brake.
