## Getting started

Tilt Rally runs in the browser at https://austegard.com/fun-and-games/tilt-rally.html. You need a phone with a motion sensor. On iOS, allow motion access when the browser asks for it.

1. Lock screen rotation in portrait before you start. The game switches to landscape on its own when it needs to, and shows a prompt telling you to turn the phone.
2. Hold the phone in portrait for the first screen and choose your options there: the track (Fjord, Quarry or Ring), the number of laps and the difficulty.
3. When the prompt appears, turn the phone to landscape. Hold it in the position you want to drive from until the start lights go out. At that moment the game sets the phone's current tilt as zero for steering.

Tilt the phone left or right to steer. Tilt the top of the phone away from you to accelerate, and towards you to brake.
