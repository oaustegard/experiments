## MEDIUM
- **Meta 2: Precomputing Reusable Knowledge for Online Harness Evolution in Long-Horizon Agents** ([2611.05139](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05139)). A frozen, offline-distilled Meta-Skill guides a separate evolver that makes KEEP/EVOLVE edits at checkpoints and discards them after each task. Terminal-Bench 2.1 81.00 vs 73.33 Meta-Harness (baseline sd ≈4.4, 60 tasks); DeepSWE 44.44 vs 37.33. Resetting edits per task matters: carrying them over drops 81.00→78.33 and 44.44→39.11. Adoptable cheaply as a separate evolver call plus per-task reset; offline stage costs $110–239 per benchmark. No code released.
