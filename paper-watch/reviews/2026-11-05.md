# paper-watch 2026-11-05

2 new papers from the Top 30 (window since 2026-11-04); both read in full, both LOW.

## LOW

- **The Trinity: Capability-Driven Recursive Self-Improvement for Multimodal Agents** ([2610.36077](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36077)), rank 26. Evolves module-based agent harnesses per capability node and routes tasks with an LLM router that retrieves similar past outcomes; that router loop (commit a choice with rationale, retrieve up to 10 similar outcomes, write back verified outcome and cost) could be tried on Muninn's model routing. The gains rest on a frozen frontier backbone and roughly 400-580M tokens of evolution per configuration, and the router saves only about 9% of tokens over random routing (Table 4). Repo: https://github.com/InfRec-Cardinal/The-Trinity.
- **Towards Agentic Robots: Model, Data, Environment, and Harness** ([2610.20442](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20442)), rank 30, 169 upvotes. Survey and taxonomy with no experiments of its own. Sec. 7.6 describes code proposing legal candidates and a fast decision model (citing TypeSafe's Jev) choosing one above a confidence threshold, which matches the existing decide-based routing and changes nothing.
