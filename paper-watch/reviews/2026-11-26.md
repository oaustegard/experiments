## LOW

- [Verifiable On-Policy Distillation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30325) (rank 17, 198 upvotes, read: full). Scales a full-vocabulary on-policy distillation gradient by the per-group correlation between verifier reward and the teacher's implicit reward (log p_T/p_ref). 4B student: 66.38 vs 64.71 for vanilla OPD averaged over 8 math benchmarks; 1.7B: 53.32 vs 50.88. No seeds or CIs, and lambda was tuned on the same AIME sets. Needs teacher logits and a frozen reference model, so it does not apply to API-routed models. For the OPSD-into-LoRA design (memory 9192e44e) it addresses the missing-reward problem only in domains with a checkable verifier; the self-distillation extension is untested. Code: https://github.com/juntongshi48/VOPD
