## LOW

- **Latent Feedback Is a Compute and Data Amplifier** ([2611.20404](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20404)), rank 8, 272 upvotes. Pretraining recipe that feeds each token's top-layer state into the next token's input; claims a 1.34-1.91x compute/data multiplier at 60M-778M. Adopting it means pretraining a modified architecture, and the paper's own control shows a ~1.2x larger plain Transformer at equal FLOPs reaches lower loss. Code: https://github.com/ifm-ai/LoRM.
- **Training Reasoning Models in Simulation to Natively Use Real Robots** ([2611.18562](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18562)), rank 30, 161 upvotes. GRPO in simulation teaches Qwen3.8-27B to emit end-effector poses from camera frames (LIBERO 1.6% to 74.4%; real robot 18/30), at ~50k H100-hours. Bears on cheap-tier routing: about a quarter of the thinking tokens per round dropped closed-loop success from 74.4% to 21.9% (one model, one benchmark). Code: https://github.com/mit-han-lab/pear.
