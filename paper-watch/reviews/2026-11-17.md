### 29. Gamer: A Generalist Vision-Language-Action Foundation Model for Long-Horizon Real-Time Game Agent ([2611.18397](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18397)), 165 upvotes
Read: full text (arxiv.org/html/2611.18397v1). Qwen3-VL-8B and Qwen3.5-35B-A3B trained on keyboard-mouse game trajectories (continued pretraining, SFT with sparse reasoning and memory summaries, segment-reward RL) and served by a custom Streaming-vLLM engine at 65 ms per step on H200 (from 1.31 s on the authors' own TP1 baseline). On one Honkai: Star Rail stage Gamer-RL reaches 83.6% of milestones against 45.5% for Gemini-3.8-Flash, and the margin over frontier models exists only in real-time play; time-paused, GPT-5.6-Sol scores about 52 against Gamer-8B-CPT's 33.9. The smallest model needs about 22 GB to serve and the SFT/RL checkpoints are not released (project page mihoyo-gamer.github.io, CC BY-NC-SA), so nothing transfers to the in-browser DOOM Arena TRM bot, and its ViZDoom result cannot be reproduced. The pattern worth copying for agent context management is writing a summary before old turns are evicted under a fixed budget (512 tokens, 20-turn window) and reasoning only at decision points.
