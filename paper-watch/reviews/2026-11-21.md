### 30. K2-Horizon: A Frontier Fleet of Open Source Language Models — [2611.21003](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21003)
150 upvotes. Read: full text (arxiv.org/html/2611.21003v1). Prior: memory e3fc0758 (Uno on M5 Max feasibility, 2026-09-07) already noted the K2-Horizon Uno checkpoints.

Six Apache-2.0 open models (0.9B dense to 375B-A23B MoE), base 0.9B/7B also released in MLX (bf16/8/4-bit) and GGUF. Uno LoRA diffusion adapters give lossless self-speculative decoding with no draft model: 7B-Uno is about 2.2x at batch 1 and 1.5x at batch 64 (H200, Nano-vLLM, tree sampler); 0.9B-Uno is about 1.2x at both (576 to 681 tok/s at c1, Table 39). Code: github.com/ifm-ai/uno.

Use: the 7B is full attention in every layer, so it sidesteps the linear-attention problem e3fc0758 flagged for Qwen3.8 and is the cleanest target for step 1 of that plan (port Psi-Spec sampling into the DFlash-MLX harness). Uno has no MLX or Apple-silicon path or numbers in the paper, so the port is still the work. Quantization note: the 3.7B and 0.9B lose 6.9 and 5.3 points at Q4_K_M (7B loses 2.1).
