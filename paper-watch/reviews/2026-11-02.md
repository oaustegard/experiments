## Medium

- **[BizBench: Unveiling Hidden Challenges in Deploying Long-Horizon Agents for Real-World Business](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20424)** (2610.20424, rank 8, read: full). 50-task stateful payments-workflow agent benchmark (789 docs, 601 tools, LLM user simulator) with deterministic process checks; harness and data at github.com/ant-intl/BizBench (now BizAgentBench) and huggingface.co/datasets/ant-intl/BizBench. Claude Opus 5 has 64.0% strict success and GPT-6 Sol 61.3%. Two findings carry over to subagent delegation. A tool-call cap of 2x the reference trace cuts DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash strict success from 43.3% to 26.7% (6.7% at 1.5x), so tight turn budgets are costly. Outcome-only checks miss process violations such as crossing a reserve floor or acting before approval. Disclosing the customer's constraints up front lifted success to 70.0%, against 50.0% for gold docs. The domain is far from Muninn and there are only 1–3 runs per model.
