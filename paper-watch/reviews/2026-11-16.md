### 11. Unbundling Multimodal Vision Encoding ([2611.17334](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17334)), 237 upvotes
Read: full text (arxiv.org/html/2611.17334v1). Controlled from-scratch study (0.6B-6.4B backbones, 224px) that splits the vision encoder into pretrained prior, dedicated vision capacity and pre-fusion placement. Dedicated capacity matters but can sit in the backbone's earliest layers; a from-scratch encoder matches a MetaCLIP-pretrained one at matched compute (1.7B, 1.04e22 FLOPs), and encoder-free needs about 3x the compute to match. No code or weights released, and every result needs a full VLM training run, so nothing changes for the pretrained in-browser encoders (MobileCLIP-S0, EG2 vision).
