## LOW

### 25. TeleChat to Xing4: Advancing Frontier Agentic Models on Domestic Chips and Frameworks ([2611.22115](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22115)), 159 upvotes
Read: full text (arxiv.org/html/2611.22115v1). Technical report on the Xing 4 series trained on Ascend 910C/MindSpore (MLA+DSA+IndexShare attention, wider-shallower MoE, DSpark speculative decoding). Every throughput number (DSpark 1.48-2.10x over MTP3 decode) is for the unreleased 285B model on Ascend or from simulation, and the open 29B-A4B gets no quality benchmarks. Server-side decoding tricks are not a lever for an API-calling agent.

### 28. ScaleVLA: Scaling Vision-Language-Action Models to 552 Billion Parameters ([2611.21607](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21607)), 150 upvotes
Read: full text (arxiv.org/html/2611.21607v1). A frozen 552B MoE VLM as encoder for a flow-matching action head; needs four H200s and releases no code or weights. The 4/10 to 9/10 robot headline is against the weakest baseline; against pi0.5 it is 9/10 vs 8/10 on 10 trials. The only transferable point is that a bigger frozen backbone helps most when labelled data is scarce.

### 29. Multi-Token Prediction Done Right ([2610.38331](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38331)), 149 upvotes
Read: full text (arxiv.org/html/2610.38331v1). Soft-MTP drops the noisy residual from the MTP gradient with a one-line straight-through change (code: github.com/JaydenTeoh/Soft-MTP). Gains at 1.2B are small and unseeded: perplexity 10.74 vs 10.89, HumanEval pass@64 43.9 vs 40.9, acceptance length +1-4%. It is a pretraining objective he would not run.

### 30. Hy-Nomas: Towards Free-form Interactive World Generation ([2610.36729](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36729)), 147 upvotes
Read: full text (arxiv.org/html/2610.36729v1). 8B autoregressive interactive video world model with released weights (huggingface.co/tencent/Hy-Nomas) that runs on one RTX 4090 via INT8 weights and KV cache plus sliding-window attention. Evaluation is 60-case pairwise human judging, and Genie 3 wins on spatial memory. Video world generation is off his path; the INT8 KV-cache-on-consumer-GPU engineering is the only reusable note.
