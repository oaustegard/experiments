## MEDIUM

### 5. Language Models Can Rewrite Their Own Weights ([2611.21885](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21885)), 326 upvotes
Read: full text (arxiv.org/html/2611.21885v1), key numbers verified by the orchestrator. Qwen3.5-27B measures a "does not stop" direction from 20 finished and 20 looping answers' hidden states and removes 5% of it from layer 8's o_proj and down_proj (W ← W − 0.05·uuᵀW). It submits an edit only when same-seed paired tests on reserved practice problems favour it. Result: +6.6 points on 490 held-out problems × 8 answers in a test fixed in advance. The first-protocol edit gains only 57.2% → 58.3%, and a corrupted copy is repaired from 0.0% to 33.0% (base 58.5%). This is the same kind of rank-one loop-direction edit as his Monad A8p o_proj edit (memory 7eb5979a). The same-seed paired acceptance test can be reused directly to vet such edits. Caveats: one session per model size behind the "emerges with scale" claim, the +6.6 edit is one of three submitted in eight sessions, part of the gain is fewer token-limit overruns, and no code is released.

## LOW

### 21. Counterfactual World Models: JEPAs That Reason About "What If" ([2611.24778](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24778)), 169 upvotes
Read: full text (arxiv.org/html/2611.24778v1). Residual reuse: the counterfactual next embedding is the observed one plus μ(z,a′) − μ(z,a) from a factual JEPA predictor, with no training. Counterfactual R² is 86–96% vs 38–46% without the outcome on four rendered simulators (code: github.com/galilai-group/counterfactual-WM). He runs no action-conditioned world model, and the validity condition needs paired outcomes to check.

### 23. Jeveration: System One Models Now Generate ([2611.24738](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24738)), 166 upvotes
Read: full text (arxiv.org/html/2611.24738v1), checked by the orchestrator. Haiku rated it MEDIUM; the orchestrator rated it LOW. This is NUS work, not TypeSafe's. A frozen Qwen3.5-4B with rank-256 LoRA picks 128 TA-TiTok image codes in one pass, conditioned on a shared random state. GenEval is 0.74 in 78 ms on an H200 (5.2B params), against 0.72 for one-step SANA-Sprint 0.6B and 0.80 for Janus-Pro-7B. No code is released. The paper is about image generation and reports nothing on Typed Decisions, so it changes nothing for the decide, haiku-decider or dettin work. Its references cite SO-Arena ([2611.15777](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15777)), a benchmark of System One models' capability and reliability, which is closer to that work.

### 28. Envisioning Agentic Robotics: A New Era of Robotic Systems ([2611.18359](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18359)), 160 upvotes
Read: full text (arxiv.org/html/2611.18359v1). A position and survey paper that defines agentic robotics as a foundation model plus a harness (memory, tools, verifier, budget, safety gate). It runs no experiments of its own and releases nothing. One caution transfers: reported success depends on how tools are exposed (EmbodiedSkillBench 92/61/85%), so any routing eval should vary model and harness separately, holding the other fixed.
