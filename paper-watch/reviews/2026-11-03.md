## Medium

- **[Recursive Self-Improvement through Multi-Agent Self-Supervision](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12176)** (2610.12176, rank 30, read: full). MASS alternates evolutionary search over orchestrator/subagent workflow prompts (roles, information handoffs, call order), generated, run and self-judged by Qwen3.6-27B, with LoRA SFT on the resulting traces; code at [github.com/SakanaAI/mass](https://github.com/SakanaAI/mass). The prompt-level workflow search is what carries over to Muninn's subagent delegation, and the ablation finds workflow optimisation, not evaluation, is the bottleneck. Win rate vs base rises from 53.9% to 69.9% over two cycles, but on private GPT-5.5-generated tasks judged by frontier models. On public benchmarks Terminal-Bench 2.0 (37.5 to 35.2) and SWE-bench Verified (62.6 to 61.1) regress, and the 1.2–1.6x headline is score per output token on four research benchmarks. A multi-agent-trace student beats a single-agent student given ~1.4x more tokens (68.3% vs 64.0%). The SFT half needs a 27B model and H100s.

## Low

- **[Training Foundation Models with a Single Algorithm](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40564)** (2610.40564, rank 20, read: full). Recasts MaxRL-style RL as weighted maximum likelihood so pretraining, SFT and RL run as one mixed-data loss; shown at 124M and 0.76B scale, code and weights released. Needs a pretraining budget. The one portable note is that replaying ~0.5–5% earlier-stage data during fine-tuning reduced forgetting.
