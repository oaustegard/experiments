### 23. [Motion Mamba 2.0: Autoregressive Diffusion Mamba for Long-Horizon Humanoid Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29897) (171 upvotes, read: full)
Humanoid (Unitree G1) motion generation that needs a 100k-update training run on a 589-hour custom corpus; quality gains are single-digit centimetres and its per-window latency is about 3x ARDY's. Two lessons transfer: write only accepted state, never speculative candidates, into persistent memory; and most of its 6.1x speedup came from CUDA-graph replay that cut dispatch count (285 ms vs 276 ms with kernel fusion added), which bears on WebGPU dispatch-count work.
