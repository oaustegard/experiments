Turn a page into markdown, on your own GPU

Drop a scan, a photo of a page or a PDF. A small OCR model finds the text lines, reads them and puts them in reading order, with headings, lists and tables, all inside this tab: the page is not uploaded anywhere. It is Indra OCR by Datalab, its weights packed to a few bits each.

How it works: a detector finds the text lines; a recognizer reads each line (a CJK, right-to-left or "world" recognizer is
  fetched the first time a line in those scripts turns up); a small transformer puts the lines in reading order and labels
  headings, lists and tables. The models run on WebGPU, ported from the
  indra-ocr package, whose output this page reproduces line for line.
  Write-up: indra-browser.

  
The model weights are Datalab's, under a modified OpenRAIL-M license (copy): free for
  research and personal use, with use restrictions in its Attachment A. The files served here are quantized derivatives
  of the originals, changed only by rounding their weights and n-gram values. Build .
