Paper Drum

Not started.

Draw a drum, then play it

  1. Draw a closed shape on white paper with a dark marker: a circle, a square, a star. Thick lines work best.
  2. Point the camera at the paper so the whole drawing is in view.
  3. Tap inside your drum with one finger. Big drums sound low, little ones sound high.

The camera sees where your finger is and the microphone hears the tap. Your GPU then rings a drum skin with exactly the shape you drew, stepping it 48,000 times a second. Nothing leaves your device.
