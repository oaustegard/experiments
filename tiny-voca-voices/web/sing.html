tiny-voca Sings

tiny-voca was trained only on speech. Here it is told how long each sound lasts and what pitch to hold, so it sings lyrics to notes you write, in any voice from the Voice Tuner. It runs in this page.

Lyrics

Notes

Hyphenate words into syllables (A-maz-ing); each line is one phrase. Write one note per syllable, in order, as NOTE/BEATS: D4/1, F#4/.5, Bb3/2. Join notes with ~ to stretch one syllable over several (B4/.5~G4/.5). A rest is r/1.

The default is the first verse of "Amazing Grace" (John Newton, 1779) to the tune "New Britain" (1829), both in the public domain. Paste your own words and notes for anything you have the rights to.
