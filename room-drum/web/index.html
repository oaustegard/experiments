Drum on the shape the camera sees

Edges in the camera image become walls in a simulated drum skin, and every enclosed shape is a drum. Tap or click inside one, or press 1 to 9 to hit the numbered drums (1 is the largest and lowest); you hear how a drum of exactly that outline rings. Walls settle where the picture holds still, so hold a hand still for a moment, or point the camera at a drawing, to shape the drums.

The skin is a grid of thousands of cells that your GPU steps forward once for every audio sample, 48,000 times a second. Bigger shapes ring lower. Headphones are best.
