## Getting started

Tilt Rally runs in the browser at https://austegard.com/fun-and-games/tilt-rally.html. You need a phone with a motion sensor.

1. Lock screen rotation in portrait. The game asks for landscape itself and prompts you to turn the phone when it needs it.
2. Open the game. On iOS, allow motion access when asked.
3. Hold the phone in portrait and choose your track, number of laps and difficulty on the first screen. Three tracks ship: Fjord, Quarry and Ring.
4. Tap 'Start engine', then turn the phone to landscape when the game prompts you.
5. Hold the phone the way you want to drive. Steering is calibrated when the start lights go out: whatever tilt the phone has at that moment counts as straight and level.

### Controls

- Tilt left or right to steer.
- Tilt the top of the phone away from you to accelerate.
- Tilt it towards you to brake.
