## Getting started

Tilt Rally runs in the browser at https://austegard.com/fun-and-games/tilt-rally.html. You need a phone with a motion sensor. On iOS, allow motion access when the browser asks for it.

1. Lock screen rotation in portrait. The game asks you to turn the phone to landscape when it needs to, so the operating system should not rotate the screen on its own.
2. Open the game with the phone held upright. The first screen has the options: track (Fjord, Quarry or Ring), number of laps and difficulty.
3. Tap 'Start engine', then turn the phone to landscape when the game prompts you.
4. Hold the phone in the position you want to drive from while the start lights count down. When the lights go out, the game takes the phone's tilt at that moment as straight ahead with no throttle.

### Controls

- Tilt left or right to steer.
- Tilt the top of the phone away from you to accelerate.
- Tilt the top of the phone towards you to brake.
