## Getting started

Tilt Rally runs in a phone browser at https://austegard.com/fun-and-games/tilt-rally.html. It needs a phone with a motion sensor.

1. Lock screen rotation in portrait. The game asks you to turn the phone to landscape when it needs it.
2. Open the game. On iOS, allow motion access when asked.
3. On the first screen, with the phone held in portrait, choose the track (Fjord, Quarry or Ring), the number of laps and the difficulty.
4. Tap **Start engine**, then turn the phone to landscape when the prompt asks you to.
5. Hold the phone the way you want to drive it. When the start lights go out, the game takes the phone's tilt at that moment as straight ahead.

### Controls

- Tilt left or right to steer.
- Tilt the top of the phone away from you to accelerate.
- Tilt it towards you to brake.
