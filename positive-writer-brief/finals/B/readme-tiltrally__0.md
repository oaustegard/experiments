## Getting started

Tilt Rally runs in a phone browser at https://austegard.com/fun-and-games/tilt-rally.html. You steer by tilting the phone, so you need a phone with a motion sensor.

### Before you start

1. Lock your phone's screen rotation in portrait. The game asks for landscape itself, with a prompt telling you when to turn the phone.
2. Open the game at the link above.
3. On iOS, allow motion access when the browser asks.

### Choosing a race

The first screen is held in portrait, and it has the race options:

- **Track:** Fjord, Quarry or Ring
- **Laps**
- **Difficulty**

When you have chosen, tap **Start engine**.

### Starting the race

When the game prompts you, turn the phone to landscape and hold it the way you want to drive. The steering is zeroed when the start lights go out, so whatever tilt the phone has at that moment counts as straight and neutral. Hold the phone steady until the lights go out.

### Controls

| Tilt | Effect |
|---|---|
| Left or right | Steer |
| Top of the phone away from you | Accelerate |
| Top of the phone towards you | Brake |
