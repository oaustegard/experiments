## Getting started

Tilt Rally runs in the browser at https://austegard.com/fun-and-games/tilt-rally.html and needs a phone with a motion sensor. On iOS, allow motion access when the game asks for it.

Lock screen rotation in portrait before you start. The game asks for landscape itself, with a prompt telling you to turn the phone.

The first screen is held in portrait, and it is where you choose the track, the number of laps and the difficulty. Three tracks ship: Fjord, Quarry and Ring.

### Your first race

1. On the first screen, set the track, laps and difficulty, then tap **Start engine**.
2. Turn the phone to landscape when the prompt asks.
3. Hold the phone the way you want to drive. When the start lights go out, the game takes the phone's tilt at that moment as neutral.

### Controls

| Tilt | Action |
|---|---|
| Left / right | Steer |
| Top of the phone away from you | Accelerate |
| Top of the phone towards you | Brake |
