## Getting started

To play Tilt Rally, open https://austegard.com/fun-and-games/tilt-rally.html on a phone that has a motion sensor. Lock screen rotation in portrait, pick your options, then turn the phone to landscape when the game asks you to.

### Before you start

- Lock your phone's screen rotation in portrait. The game shows its own prompt when it needs landscape, so leaving auto-rotate on would make the screen turn twice.
- On iOS, tap allow when the game asks for motion access. The game can't read your tilt until you allow it.

### Options

The first screen, held in portrait, has the race options:

- **Track:** Fjord, Quarry or Ring
- **Laps**
- **Difficulty**

### Starting a race

1. Set your options and tap **Start engine**.
2. Turn the phone to landscape when the prompt appears.
3. Hold the phone the way you want to hold it while you drive. When the start lights go out, the game records the phone's tilt at that moment as neutral, and you steer from that position.

### Controls

| Tilt | Action |
|---|---|
| Left or right | Steer |
| Top of the phone away from you | Accelerate |
| Top of the phone towards you | Brake |
