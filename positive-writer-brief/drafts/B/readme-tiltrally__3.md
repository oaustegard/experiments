## Getting started

Open https://austegard.com/fun-and-games/tilt-rally.html on a phone with a motion sensor, lock screen rotation in portrait, and pick your options before you turn the phone. On iOS, allow motion access when the game asks for it.

### Before you start

- **Lock rotation in portrait.** The game asks for landscape itself and shows a prompt telling you to turn the phone. If rotation is unlocked, the screen will flip on its own.
- **Choose your options on the first screen.** This screen is held in portrait. It is where you set the track, the number of laps and the difficulty. Three tracks ship: Fjord, Quarry and Ring.

### Starting a race

1. Set track, laps and difficulty, then tap **Start engine**.
2. Turn the phone to landscape when the prompt asks.
3. Hold the phone the way you want to drive. The game reads its current tilt as neutral when the start lights go out, so hold it steady until then.

### Controls

| Tilt | Action |
|---|---|
| Left / right | Steer |
| Top of the phone away from you | Accelerate |
| Top of the phone towards you | Brake |
