## Getting started

Tilt Rally runs in a phone browser at https://austegard.com/fun-and-games/tilt-rally.html. It needs a phone with a motion sensor.

1. Lock screen rotation in portrait. The game asks for landscape itself when the race is about to start, and an unlocked screen would rotate before it asks.
2. Open the game. On iOS, allow motion access when asked.
3. Choose your options on the first screen, holding the phone upright: the track (Fjord, Quarry or Ring), the number of laps and the difficulty.
4. Tap **Start engine**, then turn the phone to landscape when the prompt asks you to.
5. Hold the phone the way you want to drive it. When the start lights go out, the game takes its current tilt as straight ahead, so that position becomes neutral.

### Controls

- Tilt left or right to steer.
- Tilt the top of the phone away from you to accelerate.
- Tilt it towards you to brake.
