## Getting started

Tilt Rally runs in the browser at https://austegard.com/fun-and-games/tilt-rally.html. It needs a phone with a motion sensor.

1. Lock screen rotation in portrait. The game asks you to turn the phone to landscape when it needs it, and an unlocked screen will rotate the page out from under you.
2. Open the game. On iOS, allow motion access when asked; without it the game cannot read the tilt.
3. Holding the phone in portrait, choose a track (Fjord, Quarry or Ring), the number of laps and the difficulty, then tap **Start engine**.
4. Turn the phone to landscape when the prompt asks you to, and hold it the way you want to drive.
5. Wait for the start lights. When they go out, the game takes the phone's tilt at that moment as straight ahead, so keep it steady until then.

### Controls

- Tilt left or right to steer.
- Tilt the top of the phone away from you to accelerate.
- Tilt the top of the phone towards you to brake.
